My Story

From Tullu Bollo
to the Web

My name is Iftiyoom Mulatu. I was born in South West Shewa Zone, Bacho Woreda, in the town of Tullu Bollo — a place that shaped my curiosity and drive.

I started my academic journey at Fitewurari Habtegiyorgis Primary School and have since pursued my passion at Jimma University, where I am studying Information Technology.

I am learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — and building projects that combine creativity with technology. My passion lies in web development and networking, and I am just getting started.
